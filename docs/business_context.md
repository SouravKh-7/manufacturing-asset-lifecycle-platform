# Business Context

## Company

A fictional multi-plant manufacturing company operates industrial production equipment across multiple factories.

The company wants a unified platform to track the complete lifecycle, operational condition, maintenance history, reliability, and health of its manufacturing assets.

## Initial Plants

- Jaipur Manufacturing Plant
- Pune Manufacturing Plant
- Chennai Manufacturing Plant

## Asset Hierarchy

Company
→ Plant
→ Production Line
→ Asset

## Initial Asset Types

- CNC Machine
- Grinding Machine
- Motor
- Pump
- Compressor
- Furnace

## Core Business Problem

Asset information is fragmented across multiple systems such as asset registers, maintenance records, sensor systems, and manual files.

The pipeline uses one stable ID for every machine and combines the source data into a single machine summary.

## Initial Objectives

1. Identify every manufacturing asset uniquely.
2. Track where each asset is installed.
3. Track machine history and maintenance work.
4. Capture operating and sensor data.
5. Measure downtime and reliability.
6. Calculate asset health.
7. Identify assets requiring attention.
8. Later predict failures and remaining useful life.
